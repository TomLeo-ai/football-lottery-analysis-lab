# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on compliant technical research and simulation review.
- Clearly separate evidence, assumptions, and opinions.
- Avoid claims that imply guaranteed outcomes, profit certainty, or official endorsement.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Requests to add official data crawlers, real purchase flows, payment flows, ticket issuing, proxy purchase, following-order, or deposit and withdrawal capabilities.
- Sharing private screenshots, credentials, tokens, or copied official assets.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate this code or the compliance boundary of the project.
